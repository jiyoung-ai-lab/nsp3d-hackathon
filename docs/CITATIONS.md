### #58 — Free-flight body-saccade kinematics & expansion-threshold trigger (consulted)
- **Paper**: Bender, J. A., & Dickinson, M. H. (2006). *Visual stimulation of saccades in magnetically tethered Drosophila*. **J. Exp. Biol.**, 209(16), 3170–3182. [DOI: 10.1242/jeb.02369](https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.02369) / [PMID 16888065](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16888065/) (verified 2026-10-31)
- **Claim used**: free-flight body saccades ≈ 90° in ≈ 70 ms; saccades evoked when an expanding object crosses a critical size threshold, timing ~independent of expansion time course. (The 35°/80 ms figures are the paper's magnetically-tethered prep, NOT free flight.)
- **Used at**: saccade kinematics reference (consulted only).
- **Code**: none — consulted, nothing copied. Our `saccadePulse` is goal-loss casting search (4 s period), explicitly a different behavior from expansion-evoked saccades.
